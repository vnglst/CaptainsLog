This is a diary from an entirely imaginary world. I visited the lantern market in Brumora with the storyteller Nera Pindle. The names in this account belong only to our invented village. The village calls its autumn procession Umbrup. Nera Pindle had also named her fish puppet Umbrup, and the little pond behind the theatre was Umbrup on the map. Zoq was the name of the puppet maker and of the boat he had carved. The word Poffle appeared on the lantern shop, the bell tower and the festival programme. We carried Umbrup past Poffle and brought Zoq to the pond. The puppet, boat and lantern all belonged to the procession, although their labels were confusing.
