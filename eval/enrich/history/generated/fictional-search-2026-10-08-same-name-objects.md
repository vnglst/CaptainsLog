This is a diary from an entirely imaginary world. I visited the lantern market in Brumora with the storyteller Nera Pindle. The names in this account belong only to our invented village. The puppet was called Vevu. The lantern was called Vevu. The small painted boat was called Vevu too. Nera had made them for the Vevu Feast. At the bridge we saw Vevu again.
