I found a handwritten booklet describing the imaginary puppets. Its headings were names rather than instructions.

The circular references were a joke in the story. We eventually found the actual puppet behind the bench. At closing time we collected the paper scraps, put the lanterns in their baskets and walked home. Nobody sold anything, nobody was keeping score, and the names were just names for imaginary props.
