This is a diary from an entirely imaginary world. I visited the lantern market in Brumora with the storyteller Nera Pindle. The names in this account belong only to our invented village. The wooden fish fell asleep halfway through the song. At closing time we collected the paper scraps, put the lanterns in their baskets and walked home. Nobody sold anything, nobody was keeping score, and the names were just names for imaginary props.
