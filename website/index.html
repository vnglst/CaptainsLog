Your thoughts stay
with you.

A journal can be personal. CaptainsLog records and processes your voice on your own computer, without sending your audio or notes to an inference service.

Local processing

Whisper transcribes your speech. Qwen cleans and organizes the text. Both run on Apple Silicon through native, on-device inference.

Offline after setup

Models download once, then recording and processing work offline. Semantic search downloads its own model on first use. Optional update checks use the network, without sending your logs.

Markdown you can keep

Completed logs are plain Markdown files in your chosen data folder. Read them in a text editor or use them with a Markdown tool such as Obsidian. The original audio and earlier processing stages are kept separately.

Why a captain’s log?

A record of where you are, what happened, and what you’re thinking about. CaptainsLog brings that idea to everyday life: a spoken journal that becomes a collection of readable notes. Recording, transcription, cleanup, and organization happen in one flow, so keeping the log takes less manual work.

What Mac do I need?

An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later. The local models need several gigabytes of storage. First launch needs an internet connection and time to download them.

Does anything leave my computer?

Your audio and notes stay local. Model setup downloads the required models, and optional Homebrew update checks refresh metadata over the network. Those checks do not send your recordings or notes. You can disable automatic checks and installation in Settings.

What does search do?

Search combines keyword matches with semantic matches to retrieve relevant log entries and excerpts. It helps you find your notes; it does not generate answers about your journal.

Can I use the terminal?

Yes. One Homebrew install includes the Mac app and the cl CLI. Use cl pipeline to record and process, cl pipeline --input memo.m4a for a file, and cl search "project architecture" to find logs. Interrupted processing can be resumed with cl resume <stem>. Read the CLI instructions.

Can I trust the generated text?

Transcription and text processing can make mistakes. Review important names, facts, and wording before relying on a log. CaptainsLog keeps the original audio and earlier stage outputs so you can compare them with the completed entry.

How is the app installed?

Through the project’s Homebrew cask. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; the cask removes macOS quarantine from the app bundle. Install only if you trust the project. Review the installation instructions.
