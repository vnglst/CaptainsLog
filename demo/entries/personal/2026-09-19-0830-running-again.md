I went for a run this morning for the first time in nearly a month. Forty minutes at an easy pace, with a walk up the hill halfway through. It was slower than I remember, but I felt better when I got home. The sensible plan is two short runs a week for now, not a training schedule that I will abandon after ten days.

Later I called Beverly Crusher. We had intended to speak in August and kept missing each other. She talked through a possible move in her medical practice, and I mostly listened. It struck me afterward how much I had missed a conversation with no agenda or deadline.

I am putting a standing call on the calendar and leaving Saturday mornings open for a run. Neither is complicated, which is probably why they are worth doing.
