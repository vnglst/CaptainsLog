We went back to Briar Ridge Reserve on Saturday because Lucas wanted another chance to use the bird book he borrowed from the library. We managed about two kilometers in two hours. He wrote down a great tit, several wood pigeons, and what he insists was a nuthatch. I am less certain about the last one, but he checked the markings carefully and made a good case.

Sofia was skeptical about the walk until she found a patch of mushrooms near the pond. She spent most of the picnic trying different angles with her camera, then showed Isabel the pictures on the way home. Isabel had packed sandwiches and coffee. I left my phone in the bag and only noticed when we were back at the car.

It was an ordinary morning in the best sense. I want to keep one weekend afternoon free of notifications instead of letting the week spill into it.
