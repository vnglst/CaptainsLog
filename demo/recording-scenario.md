# Demo video recording scenario

Aim for **2–3 minutes**. Follow this sequence in the fresh demo, cutting out processing waits.

1. **Show the library — 10 seconds**  
   Start on **Logs**. Pause so viewers can see the titles, summaries, and tags.

2. **Open the Enterprise log — 20 seconds**  
   Open **enterprise-vrije-dag-sensorcontrole**. Show the summary and tags, then slowly scroll through the Dutch transcription.

3. **Play the original recording — 10 seconds**  
   Press Play, listen briefly, seek forward, then pause.

4. **Copy the note — 5 seconds**  
   Click **Copy transcription** and pause on the **Copied** confirmation.

5. **Search your notes — 15 seconds**  
   Click **All entries**. Search for **“holodeck”**, pause on the matching passage, then open the result. Rehearse this once beforehand so the search model is ready.

6. **Filter the library — 10 seconds**  
   Return to Logs and clear search. Open **All categories**, deselect **Personal**, then choose **All categories** to restore the library.

7. **Show personalization — 20 seconds**  
   Open **Context**. Show the Starfleet background, the name corrections, and the configurable categories.

8. **Record a new log — 20 seconds**  
   Return to Logs and start recording. Say:

   > Engineering log. During the encounter at El-Adrel, I helped monitor the communications systems. The universal translator translated the Tamarian words, but we still couldn’t understand their meaning.

   Pause recording briefly, resume, then say:

   > Next time, I want to pay closer attention to the stories behind the words.

   Stop recording.

9. **Show processing and the result — 20 seconds, with a cut**  
   Show a processing stage briefly, then cut ahead to the finished note. Open it and pause on its generated title, summary, tags, and cleaned text.  
   Add a caption during the cut: **“Processing locally — wait shortened.”**

10. **Finish on the completed note — 5 seconds**  
    Leave the note visible. Suggested closing caption: **“Your thoughts, organized. On your Mac.”**
